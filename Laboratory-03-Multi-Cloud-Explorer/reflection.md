# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

## Mission Reflection

Throughout this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. I was impressed by how many different services AWS provides and how these services can be used for different purposes such as computing, storage, networking, databases, security, and application development. I also learned that AWS can be used by different types of organizations, from small startups that are just beginning to large companies that need powerful and reliable cloud infrastructure. Its flexibility and scalability make it easier for organizations to adjust their resources depending on their workload and number of users.

While studying AWS, **Microsoft Azure**, and **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**, I noticed that the three platforms have many similarities. All of them provide services for cloud computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management. They also allow users and organizations to increase or decrease their resources depending on their needs. These cloud platforms can be used for many different purposes, including hosting websites and applications, storing files and data, managing databases, performing data analytics, and supporting large business systems. Even though they offer similar services, each provider has its own strengths and features that can make it more suitable for certain types of organizations.

I also learned that **business requirements are very important when choosing a cloud platform**. An organization should not simply choose a provider because it is popular. Instead, it should consider factors such as budget, existing technology, performance, security, scalability, location, and the skills of its IT staff. For example, a company that already uses many Microsoft products and services may find Azure easier to integrate with its existing systems. On the other hand, a company that focuses heavily on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, or Kubernetes may find Google Cloud more suitable. AWS can also be a good choice for organizations that need a wide variety of cloud services and flexible options.

Acting as a **Cloud Solutions Consultant** during this mission helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider requires careful consideration. I learned that there is no single cloud platform that is automatically the best for every organization. The right choice depends on the client's specific needs, goals, budget, existing infrastructure, and future plans. Comparing similar services between AWS, Azure, and GCP helped me understand the differences between the platforms and how their services can be used to solve different real-world problems.

The **client recommendations** also helped me understand how cloud platforms can be matched to different situations. For example, AWS was recommended for a startup and a global e-commerce company because of its scalability and wide range of services. Azure was a good choice for a university that already uses Microsoft technologies, while GCP was suitable for an AI research company because of its AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes capabilities. This activity showed me that understanding the client's situation is just as important as understanding the technical features of each cloud provider.

The **Linux server investigation** was another useful part of the mission. I learned how basic Linux commands can be used to check important information about a server, such as the operating system, CPU, memory, and disk space. Commands like `cat /etc/os-release`, `lscpu`, `free -h`, and `df -h` provided useful information that could help when planning to move a server to the cloud. This helped me see how information from an existing server can be used to decide what type of cloud resources may be needed.

My **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** also improved after completing this mission. I added my research about AWS, Azure, and GCP, comparison tables, cloud platform recommendations, decision-making information, and the Linux server investigation. Organizing these activities in GitHub helped me keep my work in one place and made the portfolio easier to follow. It also gave me experience in presenting technical information in a clear and organized way.

Overall, this mission gave me a better understanding of **cloud computing and how cloud platforms are used in real-world situations**. I learned that cloud computing is not only about using online servers and storage but also about choosing the right services based on an organization's needs. I also gained a better understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP and the different advantages they offer. Most importantly, this mission helped me develop a better way of thinking about cloud solutions and showed me how technical knowledge can be used to help organizations make better decisions.
